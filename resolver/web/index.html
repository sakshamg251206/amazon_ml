How it works and how well

Trained on US and India records, evaluated out-of-fold by state partition and on a held-out test split that adds France. All numbers on this page come from this deployment's own training report.

Pipeline

Seven stages, from raw text to F0.5-optimal decisions. Each is a module in resolver/.

NormaliseScripts to ASCII, legal forms stripped, house numbers and address parts extracted.
PartitionLabel-free state/region inference: aliases learned from the reference itself.
BlockTF-IDF top-5 per record inside its region, plus country-wide exact keys.
Featurise54 features: fuzzy, numeric, competition between candidates, adaptive vocabulary.
Stage 1LightGBM pair scorer.
Stage 2+10 collective / twin-graph features; mean of LightGBM, ExtraTrees and MLP.
DecodeEach record to its best reference, then expected-F0.5 per reference.

Blocking

Candidate generation sets the ceiling: a pair never retrieved can never be matched.

On the real competition data this design scored 0.9614 macro F0.5 out-of-fold (414k references, 19 held-out states), up from 0.595 for the exact-key rule, with blocking recall 0.969; later legal-form features reached 0.9655. See DECISIONS.md for every experiment, including the rejected ones.
